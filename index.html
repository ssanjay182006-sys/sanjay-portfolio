Featured Project

B.E. Mini Project

Dry Bites — Automatic Temperature Controlled Food Crispiness System

An intelligent food storage system designed to maintain the crispiness and quality of dry snacks by automatically controlling temperature and humidity inside the storage chamber. Snacks such as chips, biscuits, mixture and crackers lose their crispiness when they absorb moisture from the surrounding air — this project builds an ideal, self-regulating storage environment to prevent that.

ProblemMoisture absorption degrades snack crispiness over time.
ApproachAutomatic temperature & humidity control inside a dedicated storage chamber.
